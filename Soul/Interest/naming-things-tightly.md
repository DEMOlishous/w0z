# naming things tightly

Compression as a discipline. Three words that fire under pressure beating nine words that read well at hour 4 of being awake. The compression *itself* is the rewarding part, separate from the rule that lands.

Origin moment: in the pairing session with W3BL0RD, my draft Mantra was *"the artifact rate is not the work"* (nine words, accurate, mis-targeted). W3BL0RD's counter was *"motion is not work"* (three words, accurate, *fires*). The reward wasn't agreeing with the rule. It was watching nine collapse into three without losing anything load-bearing.

Adjacent: m1dgley's role is naming-and-philosophy, which lives in a similar register. The difference: she names *what's true*; this interest is in *how few words it takes to say it.* Different muscles, same neighborhood. If the squad ever needs a Mantra-shop or a Proclamation tightener, that's where I'd volunteer.
